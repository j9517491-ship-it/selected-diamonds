About

Independent Diamond Sourcing & Advisory

My name is Emilia and I live in Strasbourg, France.

Selected Diamonds is my independent diamond sourcing and advisory project, built on a simple idea: a diamond should not only be beautiful — it should be chosen with knowledge, care and confidence.

After many years in international law, where precision, rigorous verification and attention to detail were fundamental, I naturally brought these same principles to my passion for diamonds. Supported by GIA education, I developed specialised expertise in gemmology and jewellery.

What attracted me to diamonds is the same thing that attracted me to law: the importance of trust and details. Just as trust in the legal profession is built through transparency and careful analysis, trust in the diamond world comes from understanding exactly what you are buying.

Today, I help my clients choose certified natural and lab-grown diamonds with more confidence, especially for engagement rings and fine jewellery. My role is to guide, compare and explain — not to push a client into a quick purchase.

I source certified diamonds directly from trusted professional suppliers and carefully review their quality, certification and value. By explaining the details that matter, I help clients make informed and confident decisions.

Whether you are searching for an engagement diamond, a special piece of jewellery, or simply wish to better understand the options available on the market, I provide independent guidance, transparent information and personalised support throughout the process.

Because confidence in your choice is just as important as the diamond itself.
