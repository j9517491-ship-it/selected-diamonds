The stone in hand, with its GIA report alongside.

A single natural oval of 1.01ct, sourced for a wedding ring. Ovals reward careful selection more than rounds do: the length-to-width ratio and the way the bow-tie shadow falls across the centre vary from stone to stone, and neither of those appears on a certificate. They have to be looked at.
