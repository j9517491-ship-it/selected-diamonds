Wedding Earrings — Pear & Marquise Set

← Back to portfolio

The four stones laid out as they would sit in the finished pair.

Four natural diamonds sourced together for a pair of wedding earrings: two pear shapes and two marquises, one of each per ear. A set like this lives or dies on how well the stones agree with each other — the two pears are matched at E colour, the two marquises at F, so the pair reads as one design rather than four separate stones.
