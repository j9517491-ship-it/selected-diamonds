Free Consultation

A conversation before a purchase.

Thirty minutes, by video, at no cost and with no obligation. A chance to ask everything you want to understand before spending a significant amount of money.

What we will cover

The video consultation is shaped around what you need. We can discuss:

  • what the 4Cs mean in practice and where you can compromise without compromising the diamond’s beauty;
  • what your budget can realistically buy in today’s market, for both natural and laboratory-grown diamonds;
  • how to understand a grading report and what it may not reveal about the stone;
  • certificates, listings, photographs or videos of diamonds you are already considering;
  • any diamonds or jewellery you have already been offered, and whether the quality and price appear fair;
  • how the sourcing process works, including costs and timescales.

The aim is to give you clarity and help you make an informed decision, without pressure to buy.

What to have ready

You don’t need to prepare anything before our call. If you already have any of the following, it may help us go a little further:

  • a rough budget range, even a broad one;
  • a preferred diamond shape or photographs of jewellery you like;
  • certificates or listings you are already considering;
  • your ring size, wrist measurement or preferred necklace length, if known.

If you don’t have any of these yet, that is completely normal. A first consultation is often where we begin answering these questions together.

Choose a time

Online booking is being set up. In the meantime, write to me and I will offer you a few times.

Email to arrange a call

Bookings are handled by Cal.com. The times you see are my genuine availability. You will receive a confirmation and a video link by email.

No obligation, and no pressure

The consultation is free because it is genuinely useful to me for you to be well informed. If you decide not to go ahead, or to buy elsewhere, that is a perfectly good outcome — you will at least know what you are looking at.

I am an independent advisor, not a retailer with stock to move. My role is to guide, compare and explain.

If a video call does not suit you, send me a message instead and we will find another way.
