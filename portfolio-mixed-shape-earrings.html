Mixed-Shape Earring Set

← Back to portfolio

Five natural diamonds sourced together for a pair of earrings in an asymmetric, multi-stone design: two matched round brilliants of 0.40ct, an emerald cut of 0.31ct, and an oval and a pear of 0.23ct each — all D colour, VS1 to VS2.

A mixed-shape set is a harder brief than a matched pair. Each cut returns light in its own way — a brilliant scintillates, a step cut gives broad flashes and long lines — so stones that measure alike on paper can still look mismatched in the ear. The work is in choosing pieces that read as one family across five different outlines.
